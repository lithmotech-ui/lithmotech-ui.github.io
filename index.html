Programmable Liquid Handling

Robotic pipetting systems designed for routine liquid transfer, microplate workflows, assay preparation, and laboratory productivity.

The problem we are solving

Many Indian laboratories still depend heavily on manual pipetting for repetitive workflows, while imported automation systems remain expensive and inaccessible for many institutions.

Manual workload

Repetitive pipetting consumes skilled human time and can increase fatigue during routine laboratory workflows.

High import cost

Automated liquid handling systems are often imported and priced beyond the reach of many Indian diagnostic, biotech, and academic labs.

Limited accessibility

Many laboratories need practical automation that is affordable, customizable, and easier to operate without deep programming expertise.

Our solution

Lithmo is building compact, programmable liquid handling robots designed for India-ready laboratory automation.

What Lithmo is building

Our first product is a prototype-stage liquid handling platform intended to automate routine pipetting workflows across common laboratory formats such as tubes, tip racks, and microplates.

How it helps labs

The system is being designed to reduce repetitive manual work, improve workflow consistency, and make programmable automation accessible to a wider base of Indian laboratories.

Product under development

Lithmo’s first product is a programmable liquid handling robot currently in MVP/prototype development.

Planned capabilities

  • Programmable X-Y-Z robotic motion
  • Automated pipette positioning
  • Software-controlled liquid transfer workflows
  • Support for routine microplate and tube-based workflows
  • Future user-friendly protocol builder for non-programmers

Current status

Lithmo is currently developing its MVP and is open to incubation support, grant-backed product development, pilot collaborations, and early laboratory discussions.

Intended applications

Lithmo’s platform is intended for routine liquid handling and sample preparation workflows across research, diagnostics, and biotechnology environments.

Diagnostic labs

Sample preparation, reagent transfer, assay setup, and repeatable plate-based workflows.

Biotech labs

Routine pipetting, serial dilution, reagent dispensing, molecular biology preparation, and screening workflows.

Academic research

Affordable automation for university labs, research groups, and early-stage life science facilities.
